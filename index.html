FAQs

Do you offer per-head pricing?

Yes, we offer both per-head packages and customized dish-based pricing.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking as early as possible to ensure availability, especially for popular dates like weddings. Typically, booking 2-3 weeks in advance is ideal, but we can accommodate last-minute requests whenever possible.

Do you Deliver food at door step for small family events?

Yes, we do offer food delivery.
